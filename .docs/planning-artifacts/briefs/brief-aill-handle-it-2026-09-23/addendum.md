# Addendum: AI'll handle it

Detail the user gave that belongs in the PRD or UX work, not in the brief itself.

## Invitation experience (from the brain dump)
- Each guest gets a personal link.
- First visit: an animation of a physical invitation (a letter/envelope) opening, which then leads into the website.
- Later visits skip the letter and go straight to the site.
- Guests are grouped into households and couples. One member (for example the father of a family) can answer for the whole group.

## Look and feel
- Elegant but cosy. Fits a wedding without being overly fancy.
- Palette: sage green, soft white, dark wood.
- Must be easy to use for guests of all ages.

## RSVP and add-ons (per guest)
- Coming or not.
- Accommodation at Hardingasete (cabins on the farmyard): the whole weekend or none.
- Friday dinner, Saturday breakfast, Sunday breakfast.
- Allergies and dietary needs.
- Prices must be shown clearly. The couple pays for Saturday lunch and dinner.
- Maybe: sign up to give a speech.

## Weekend programme (venue: Hardingasete, Hardanger; wedding on Saturday 27 May 2028)
- Friday: mountain hike during the day for early arrivals. Evening: treasure hunt (rebus), then sauna and hot tub.
- Saturday: optional yoga, then the wedding. The couple hosts lunch and dinner.
- Guests are encouraged to stay the whole weekend.
- Hardingasete provides the venue, all meals and the cabin accommodation.

## Content pages
- About the couple.
- Important notes: child-free wedding; keep phone use to a minimum.
- Practical info: address, times, dress codes, transport tips.
- Gifts: we know the wedding may be expensive to attend and we don't expect gifts, but there is a wish list for anyone who wants one.
- Guests can send questions to the couple.

## Course context (IBE160 Programmering med KI)
- Deadline: mid-December 2026. Solo project. Must be full-stack with a database.
- A reflection report (3000–5000 words) is also required. It covers the AI-assisted process phase by phase and requires **all prompts to be saved**. It also covers tools and technology, technical and AI-specific challenges, a critical assessment of AI's impact, ethics (responsibility, transparency, learning), and data security and privacy (what data was shared with AI tools).
- Implication: the planning artifacts from this BMad run are evidence for report section 2.4 (Phase 1: planning).
- Confirmed 2026-09-26: an AI feature inside the app is not a course requirement. AI use is covered through the development process (prompt log, BMad).

## Payments
- Payment happens outside the app: guests pay the couple by bank transfer or Vipps, and the couple pays Hardingasete.
- The site shows what each guest owes and how to pay. The couple marks who has paid in the admin view.

## Guests
- About 70 guests. A few may not speak Norwegian.

## Admin (the couple and helpers)
- Overview: who is coming and who has not answered; meal counts per meal (Fri dinner, Sat breakfast, Sun breakfast); beds needed; allergies with names; who owes what and who has paid; guest questions; speech sign-ups; expected arrival time per guest; Friday hike interest.
- Link tracking: see which households have opened their link.
- The couple creates guests and households, then sends each personal link manually via Messenger, SMS or email.
- Full override: edit any answer (also after the lock), remove guests, move people between households.
- Users: the couple (full access). Possibly the toastmaster, maid of honour and best man. Speech sign-ups should reach the toastmaster.
- Decided: all admin users get full access; guests consent via a checkbox (see Later decisions).

## RSVP rules
- The RSVP deadline locks answers. A guest who tries to change after the deadline sees a message to contact the couple.
- Additional RSVP fields: expected arrival time; joining the Friday hike (yes/no/maybe).

## Later decisions
- Guest questions are forwarded to the couple's email (must-have for V1). Open question: whether sending email costs anything.
- Admin access: full access for all admin users. Guests consent via a checkbox that names who can see their information.
- AI in the site (2026-09-26): removed. The guest Q&A assistant and AI cabin/bed placement were dropped because running AI costs money and is not a course requirement. Guest questions go through the "ask us" form only.
- Language: Norwegian UI in V1. An English version, switched with a language button at the top, is a stretch goal. Until then, non-Norwegian guests ask the couple directly.
- Gift list: form undecided (static list, external link, or reservable gifts).
- Possible post-wedding keepsake (photo sharing, thank-you message) if time allows.
