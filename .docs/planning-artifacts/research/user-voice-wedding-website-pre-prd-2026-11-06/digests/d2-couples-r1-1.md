Scope note: Accessed 2026-11-06. Reddit could not be read directly. Fetches to Reddit mirrors returned HTTP 403, and `site:reddit.com` searches returned no Reddit threads. Reddit voices below come only from search-result snippets and press retellings, and they are marked low confidence. Most first-hand couple voices come from WeddingWire and The Knot forums, which are often 2018–2023 (flagged as older). Product reviews (Trustpilot) are current (2026). ## Jobs-to-be-done (synthesis)
1. When the caterer or venue deadline approaches, I want a reliable count of who is coming to each event and meal, with dietary needs, so that I can send final numbers without guessing.
2. When guests haven't replied, I want to see exactly who is missing and contact them quickly, so I don't spend a week chasing.
3. When I invite a household, I want them to answer once, for exactly the people invited, so that plus-ones and extra names don't break my headcount.
4. When guests pay for cabins or meals, I want to know who owes what and who has paid without a separate Venmo/PayPal spreadsheet, so I don't have to awkwardly chase money.
5. When helpers (partner, mother, maid of honour, toastmaster) assist, I want to give them access without sharing my password, and without giving them more access than they need.

## Unmet needs / requirement implications
- **Personal per-household links instead of name search.** This removes the exact-match lookup failures, lockouts, surname leaks and strangers RSVPing. Evidence: Zola docs plus WeddingWire threads.
- **Fixed invited-member list per household.** Allow per-person yes/no and no free-text additions. Any plus-one should be a named, pre-authorised slot. Evidence: plus-one/extra-guest threads, "The Smith Family".
- **Per-event and per-day attendance and meal choice** for a three-day weekend, plus a per-person allergy field. The admin view should export or show counts in the caterer's format, and the RSVP deadline should be set about 2 weeks before the caterer deadline (default 1–4 weeks out).
- **Non-responder list** with a contact method (phone/e-mail) for one-click chasing. Optionally, an explicit "treat as no after deadline" status.
- **Change after deadline.** The admin can edit or override replies (the couple handles late changes). Guests are locked out after the deadline, or edits are flagged.
- **Payment tracking.** Show the amount owed per household (cabin + chosen meals), with a paid / not-paid status set manually by the admin. Show prices transparently up front, because etiquette risk ("tacky", "nickel and dimed") is lower when costs are disclosed and lodging is optional.
- **Bed/cabin overview** with an optional "staying elsewhere" choice.
- **Role-limited helper access.** This is a known gap. Even a single read-only role for the toastmaster or MC would beat password sharing.
- **No paywalls or upsells** for group messaging (a Joy pain point). Treat it as a differentiator only, not a requirement for a single-wedding site.
