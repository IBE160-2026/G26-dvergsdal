## Acceptance verdict

**accepted-with-open-items** (declared criteria). Evidence against the epic's own criteria in [epic-2-context.md](epic-2-context.md):

- *One person answers for their household on a phone, step by step:* steps 1–6 and the check page are built and tested (2.1–2.5), including the du/dere wording, the day rules, the consent rules and the running total.
- *Reliability («a save either succeeds and says so, or fails clearly and keeps everything entered; a double tap, a changed price or a parallel send never loses or doubles an answer»):* `send_answer` with the `Submission` key, the revision check and the total snapshot (2.6a–2.6c), with tests that name each row of the I/O matrix.
- *Allergies stored only with consent:* 2.6b, covered again across a household change in 2.6c and 2.9.
- *Receipt, payment and status:* 2.7, 2.8; the copy buttons have an empty live region per row.
- *An interrupted answer is kept:* 2.9.
- *No unfinished stories:* `pending_stories` is empty.

Not accepted outright because: no real guest or phone browser has run the form (the epic's own «under 10 minutes, alone» goal is unmeasured), `wedding/rsvp/views.py` is a 1,528-line module, three px breakpoints and the Impeccable pass are carried from Epic 1, and there are Rikke-only checks (action 1). None of these blocks Epic 3, which counts the stored answers; Epic 3 can start.
